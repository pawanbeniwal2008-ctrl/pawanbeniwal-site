Approach

How an engagement runs

  • Listen first. Understand the decision, the decision-maker and the constraints.
  • Ground in evidence. Combine data, field realities and prior programs.
  • Make it usable. Deliver briefs, trackers and training that people actually use.
  • Stay through delivery. Monitor, learn and adjust rather than hand over and vanish.
